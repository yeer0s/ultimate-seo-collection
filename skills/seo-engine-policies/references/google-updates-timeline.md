## How to use this file

1. **Before writing any before/after**, check the window against the *data-reliability* rows. A metric that was mis-logged for eleven months does not become comparable because the dates line up.
2. **Before attributing a traffic move to your own change**, check the core/spam list for an overlapping rollout. See skill `seo-technical` › incident-triage § Classify the incident first.
3. **Before recommending a rich result or a report**, check the retirement rows. Recommending FAQ markup for SERP benefit in 2026 is a dated-fact failure, not a judgement call.
4. **Bump `last_verified` when you re-check a row.** A date verified once is not verified forever.
