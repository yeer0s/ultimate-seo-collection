## Agentic SEO — what the community adds and where it overclaims

**Superseded for the community itself (2026-09-22):** the agentic-SEO community has since been collected and read directly — see its primary corpus layer above, which confirms overclaim patterns 1 and 3 from inside the community. This section remains the trade-press view. Plainly: that community had no indexed third-party footprint. Nine searches, including its literal name, produced no page naming it, quoting a thread, or giving a member count; the community guides listing ~20 SEO communities omit it. Everything in this section stands on trade-press and vendor discourse about "agentic SEO", not on what the community says. Read it as "the agentic conversation", and re-source it if the community is ever read directly.

What the discourse adds: the workflow catalogue in Tactics 63–73 (agent loops over GSC/crawl/SERP data · ahrefs.com/blog/agentic-seo/), the MCP tooling layer listed under Tooling folklore, and one useful reframing of the deliverable — 10–15 prioritised fixes made directly in the repo instead of the 50-page PDF · stormy.ai.

Overclaim patterns:
1. Autonomy — the claim of a fully autonomous end-to-end loop that audits, fixes and validates is made only by the vendors selling it (Nuwtonic, AgenticSEO, Agentive SEO on G2). Ahrefs' own guide: agents reason the way chatbots do and make the same inference errors, and fail more on longer workflows · https://www.g2.com/products/nuwtonic/discuss
2. Fabricated metrics that look real — invented volumes and difficulty, drift between sessions, partly sourced answers mixing real SERP reads with made-up numbers. Any pipeline without a real-data MCP behind every number inherits this · contextbolt.com ; spyfu.com
3. Scale as a virtue — the March 2026 enforcement hit exactly the pipelines agentic tooling makes easy (templated pages, 50–500 posts/day, multilingual multiplication), 50–80% losses, ~6-month recovery. Agentic content at volume is the 2026 name for the thing that got burned · digitalapplied.com
4. "LLM ranking improvements" as a service line — white-label press-release language; Mueller's acronym warning applies · barchart.com ; searchengineland.com
5. Prompt-volume / "AI search volume" dashboards — extrapolated from tiny panels, a mirage metric · fatjoe.com

Honest uses, all under human review: GSC analysis automation (deltas, cannibalisation, trend flags), log parsing, crawl diffing between releases, brief drafting from a real top-10 read. This is the study's "you cannot automate this" finding restated from the other side: the automatable part is data stitching; the part that decides what a comparison page should say, whether a figure is right, and whether a change ships is not, and the study's corpus has no controlled evidence that it ever was.

## Comparison-site specifics (mowei-class sites)

All `[unvalidated house heuristic]`: folded from the Astra and Fable model reviews, not from a practitioner corpus; none has a study entry, and each is a hypothesis for the six-part recipe.

- Compare total cost under explicit customer scenarios — introductory periods, recurring fees, eligibility, contract constraints — never headline price alone.
- Disclose market coverage, excluded providers, commissions, sponsored placements, ranking logic and tie-breaks on the surface the user reads, not in a methodology footer.
- Publish a reproducible "why this offer ranks here" per row; preserve historical offer versions so a ranking can be re-derived after the offer changes.
- Keep personalised calculator states out of crawl traps (parameterised URLs, per-session results) while keeping useful public scenario pages indexable; monitor the eligible/indexed ratio per template weekly.
- Offer-expiry handling: expired offers get an explicit state and a successor link, not a silent 404 or a stale "best price"; availability and variants stay consistent between feed and page.
- Price staleness guard in titles and snippets: dynamic price messaging only with a freshness check behind it — the study's static-price entries are AVOID (#157 −15%, #158 −7%) and its dynamic variant `[measured: MAINSTREAM #155]` is a different treatment; details in `titles-and-serp-tests.md`.
- Trigger refreshes from regulatory events (ERSE tariff updates, OE, IRS tables, ANACOM, BdP, ASF) rather than traffic decay; reviewer-of-record and effective-date stamp on every calculator; ALGORITHM_VERSION visible; link /correcoes from the surface.
- Measure completed comparisons, qualified applications, acceptance and realised revenue — not affiliate clicks; lower CTR is acceptable when misleading clicks disappear.
- mowei's linkable asset is its own data: tariff deltas, switching statistics, IRS bracket effects, published as reproducible reports with coverage limits stated.
- Answer real r/portugal and r/literaciafinanceira questions with disclosure; measure as brand-search lift, not links. YouTube walkthroughs of the calculators with the transcript on-page.
