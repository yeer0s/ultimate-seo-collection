## How to read

- **Marks are counts, not verdicts.** MAINSTREAM = published ≥3 times (or twice, both controlled), never "proven". `n` = independent samples, `c` = controlled; `?` = no figure recorded in this skill's ledger parts (their catalogue lines print mark and headline only): quote the whole mark with its `?` (`c=?`), never drop or fill it.
- **Repetition is not proof.** A score records agreement, not a measurement; a claim repeated across blogs or GitHub skills is one claim. Practitioner lines stay `[practitioner]`.
- The verdict grades the claim as the field states it: AVOID mark or a contradicting engine doc → `evidence argues against (AVOID)`; spam class → `policy-forbidden`; bundle or c=0 → `measured, direction only`; isolated controlled tests → `measured, size transfers nowhere`; sources disagree → `contested`.
- No published figure is a forecast for your site. Absent from the ledger = "no published measurement found", never "does not work".
- Finding `#N`: the catalogue (#1–#182) is [evidence-and-tactics-2](evidence-and-tactics-2.md); the shortlist is [-1](evidence-and-tactics-1.md); AVOID and IT DEPENDS entries are in [-3](evidence-and-tactics-3.md); title and SERP tests with samples / controlled are in [titles-and-serp-tests](titles-and-serp-tests.md). Practitioner lines come from the parts listed in [practitioner-index](practitioner-index.md).
- "Field errors" (errors repeated across popular SEO skills, REPORT §6), the Unverified list and the Sources are in [part 2](works-vs-doesnt-2.md).
