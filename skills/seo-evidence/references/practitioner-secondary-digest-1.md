## Consensus canon

Advice repeated across many threads, deduped. Format: claim · caveat · source as the digest gives it.

1. Link quality and relevance beat volume; one link from a trusted site with a real audience outranks fifty directory links. · "Trusted" is judged by the linking page's own organic traffic, not DA/DR. · flow20.com (scores not visible) · Study bridge: editorial pitching `[measured: MAINSTREAM #8]`.
2. Directory submissions and reciprocal link exchanges are outdated and penalty-prone. · Local citation sites are still accepted as "starter" links for a brand-new domain — both positions coexist. · same URL.
3. Authority accrues at the page level, not just the domain; seek links from pages that themselves rank. · This is a general-community moderator's house position; the DR-first counter-position is `[memory]`, see the last section. · weblinkr.net
4. Pick ONE of Semrush/Ahrefs; Screaming Frog is the crawler; GSC beats any paid estimate for your own site. · The same score-51 / 133-comment general-practitioner thread's top answer names SE Ranking as the budget pick and warns against Search Atlas's metrics. · mpgone.com
5. Internal linking is the most under-used on-site lever: 2–3 contextual links per post, no sitewide footer/sidebar links to cornerstone pages. · Suspicion of automated internal-linking plugins is `[memory]`. · weblinkr.net · Study bridge: `[measured: MAINSTREAM #84, #86]`; note the study's only footer entry is a small positive `[measured: EXPERIMENTAL #95, +5%]`, so the footer half of the folk rule is untested, not confirmed.
6. Topical authority: go deep on one narrow topic first; pillar + cluster; one target keyword per page. · That "topical authority" names no confirmed ranking system is `[memory]`. · weblinkr.net; https://tdcontent.substack.com/p/how-to-build-topical-authority-in · Study bridge: `[measured: MAINSTREAM #33, #40]`.
7. New sites need 3–12 months; launch alone brings no visitors. · Posts reporting six empty months usually reduce to zero links and zero demand `[memory]`. · weblinkr.net.
8. Blog in a subfolder, never a subdomain. · Google says either works; the practitioner preference is `[memory]`. No study entry. · weblinkr.net.
9. Judge advice by the account: long-standing high-scoring members with visible history; read several replies before trusting one. · powerfulcombo: both communities are sceptical by culture. · flow20.com; mpgone.com.
10. Several simultaneous "traffic dropped" posts = an update, not your site; check before touching anything. · The professional practitioners' addition (match GSC dates to Google's status dashboard first) is `[memory]`. · flow20.com.
11. ChatGPT cannot give search volumes; it fabricates plausible numbers. · Brainstorming and intent clustering with it are accepted; the number is the problem. · a general-practitioner thread, as given by https://contextbolt.com/blog/chatgpt-keyword-research/.
12. llms.txt: harmless, maybe useful later, replaces nothing — the pragmatist middle across 38 threads in four communities. · The loudest general-practitioner camp calls it a fix without a problem (Mar 2026). · https://powerfulcombo.com/blog/is-llms-txt-worth-it/ · Study bridge: `[measured: MAINSTREAM #68, #71 — 0 controlled, range −19.7% to +81%]`; `[documented: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide, 2026-10-11]` Google Search does not use it.
13. Schema for the rich-result/CTR effect, not as a ranking factor. · The FAQ half is stale: FAQ rich results gone 7 May 2026 `[documented: https://developers.google.com/search/updates, 2026-10-11]`; the staleness caveat itself was `[memory]`. · weblinkr.net · Study bridge: `[measured: MAINSTREAM #135, #138, #141]`.
14. CWV are real but small; "tiebreaker" is the folk word. · The professional practitioners' view (CWV work pays in UX and ad revenue, not rank) is `[memory]`. · https://www.corewebvitals.io/core-web-vitals/seo · Study bridge: `[measured: MAINSTREAM #131, #132]`.
15. Disavow: do nothing without a manual action; "toxic backlink" reports are tool-generated fear. · Google's docs say misuse can harm, and also allow disavow where a considerable number of spammy links likely will cause a manual action and cannot be removed `[documented: https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/2648487, 2026-10-11]`. · https://seotldr.substack.com/p/seo-tldr-12-5-steps-to-diagnose-ranking ; https://ahrefs.com/blog/toxic-backlink-disavowal/ · Study bridge: `[measured: IT DEPENDS #13]`.
16. E-E-A-T is not something you add claims about; bylines and author boxes are not a lever by themselves. · weblinkr.net calls the E-E-A-T story invented; the 2026 recovery write-ups say the opposite — live disagreement, see Contrarian 3. · https://weblinkr.net/blog/weblinkr/ · Study bridge: `[measured: MAINSTREAM #60, #61]` — #60's figure is AI citations from 1 controlled test of 7, on one site; on YMYL, bylines are a trust requirement regardless, with no number attached.
17. Reddit threads are now a SERP feature; treat them as such, don't try to outrank them. · Reddit is weak on transactional (8%) and branded-SaaS (6%) queries — target those. · https://thestacc.com/blog/reddit-google-visibility/
18. Agents belong in the "stitching layer" (pull GSC + crawl + SERP, prioritise, draft); humans keep the judgement. · Vendor + trade-press consensus standing in for the agentic-SEO community, which surfaced nothing. · https://ahrefs.com/blog/agentic-seo/ ; https://searchengineland.com/ai-agents-seo-practical-workflow-walkthrough-469607

## Contrarian takes worth knowing

1. Adopt llms.txt early — robots.txt too began as an ignored convention that practitioners made stick (Aug 2025) · powerfulcombo.com
2. GEO is just SEO (GEO-native communities) — if nobody mentions you anywhere, schema and llms.txt aren't your first problem; entities and mentions drive citations · same URL
3. weblinkr.net's E-E-A-T-is-a-myth line, explicitly against the industry narrative and so-called GEO disinformation · weblinkr.net/blog/weblinkr/ ; contrast https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/scaled-content-abuse-google-march-update-ai-pages-decimated (verified bylines, original research as recovery)
4. Start with thin pages, then build out the ones that rank — the reverse of ten pillar guides before launch · weblinkr.net
5. Don't over-prioritise sitemaps; crawlable internal links matter more · same URL
6. Your judgement, later a more experienced one, as the only tool; Google/Bing tools + AWStats · mpgone.com
7. AIO "apocalypse" is over-measured — Chartbeat data has search referrals to publishers holding · https://www.practicalecommerce.com/?p=1559076 (snippet) ; https://seotldr.substack.com/p/gpt5-makes-seo-irreplicable-google
8. Prompt-volume metrics are a mirage, extrapolated from tiny panels · https://fatjoe.com/blog/tracking-prompt-volume/
9. Reddit is fading from ChatGPT citations (~5% share vs 46.7% in Perplexity, from Sep 2025) — posting on Reddit for GEO is Perplexity/AIO advice · https://www.axios.com/2026/08/20/chatgpt-reddit-citations-geo-strategy ; thestacc.com
10. "Agentic" is mostly a vendor label — the only entities using the phrase are platforms and a white-label press release · https://www.g2.com/sellers/agenticseo ; https://www.barchart.com/story/news/36444516/white-label-seo-agency-expands-reseller-marketing-services-to-include-agentic-seo-processes-and-llm-ranking-improvements

## What practitioners report FAILED

Each with the condition under which it failed. Newcomer-mistake shapes ("I did X and lost 80%") are merged in.

1. Disavowing tool-flagged "toxic" links — hurt one page slightly, maybe helped one, no effect on a third; condition: no manual action existed · ahrefs.com/blog/toxic-backlink-disavowal/
2. Buying a link package — a buyer's $8,400 bought an 86% toxic backlink profile; condition: vendor-sourced links · https://dev.to/techresolve/solved-linkdaddy-review-8400-spent-86-toxic-backlink-profile-19b9
3. $60k over a year of SEO, nothing back — condition: no promotion, wrong page-level targeting · weblinkr.net (thread named, URL not given)
4. Site migration → traffic plummeted — condition: 302s instead of 301s, chains, everything to the homepage, noindex/robots block left on, unmapped URLs · weblinkr.net ; https://www.seocompaniesreviewed.com/blog/seo-mistakes-site-migrations-avoid · Study bridge: `[measured: MAINSTREAM #133]` is the survivor's story — nobody publishes the migration that lost 40%.
5. Parasite / site-reputation-abuse subfolders — Forbes /advisor/, /health/ reportedly hit zero; CNN Underscored, USA Today, Fortune, WSJ Buy Side, Time Stamped de-ranked Nov 2024; Nov 2025 tightening removed first-party oversight as a defence · https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-strengthens-policy-against-site-reputation-abuse/533018/ ; https://www.capconvert.com/learn/blog/google-site-reputation-abuse-18-months ; https://seojuice.com/blog/site-reputation-abuse/ · EEA update: see next section.
6. Programmatic / templated pages (March 2026) — city/product-name swaps, AI translation into 20–50 languages, 50–500 unreviewed AI articles a day; reported 50–80% drops, ~6-month recovery · digitalapplied.com
7. HCU-hit small sites (Sep 2023) largely did not recover — a practitioner tracked ~400; partial lifts only · https://surferseo.com/blog/recover-from-helpful-content-update ; https://www.practicalecommerce.com/how-to-recover-from-googles-helpful-content-update
8. Category/tag bloat left indexed — condition: pages with no topical value · weblinkr.net · Note: the study's only tag-page entry runs the other way `[measured: MAINSTREAM #152, create tag pages]` — a genuine IT DEPENDS on whether the tag has demand.
9. Hreflang — 67% of implementations across 374,756 domains had errors; the failure is silent · https://ahrefs.com/blog/hreflang-study · No study entry.
10. ChatGPT numbers as keyword data — confident volumes for nonexistent keywords, drift between sessions · contextbolt.com ; https://www.spyfu.com/blog/dont-risk-your-work-on-imaginary-data/
11. Astroturfed Reddit threads for GEO — ban exposure, possible penalisation · https://wellows.com/blog/reddit/
12. Panic changes mid-rollout instead of waiting for the update to finish · https://dev.to/synergistdigitalmedia/googles-december-2025-core-update-the-recovery-playbook-nobodys-talking-about-39ce
13. Confusing "rankings up, traffic down" (AIO absorption) with a penalty · https://ahrefs.com/blog/website-traffic-down
14. Blaming the site for a drop that was a broad update — never checked whether others fell too · flow20.com
15. Feeding an agent a 500k-row crawl — it can silently drop rows, invent patterns or stall; longer workflows fail more · ahrefs.com/blog/agentic-seo/
16. One stale API in an agent pipeline contaminating hundreds of downstream data points · https://searchengineland.com/guide/agentic-ai-in-seo (snippet)
17. Letting an agent run unsupervised on client-facing output; a fabricated metric was plausible enough to get through a quick review · ahrefs.com ; searchengineland.com

## 2025–2026: AI Overviews, core updates, Reddit-in-SERP, GEO, site-reputation

AI Overviews / AI Mode. Top-ranking page loses ~34.5% of clicks when an AIO is present `[vendor]` (300k-keyword study, snippet · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_Overviews); zero-click share 56% → 69% May 2024 → May 2025 · https://www.searchenginejournal.com/impact-of-ai-overviews-how-publishers-need-to-adapt/556843/. Publisher losses reported: Digital Trends −97%, ZDNet −90%, The Verge −85% `[vendor]` · https://ahrefs.com/blog/biggest-traffic-losers/ ; CNN ~−30%, HuffPost/BI ~−40% · https://www.adexchanger.com/publishers/the-ai-search-reckoning-is-dismantling-open-web-traffic-and-publishers-may-never-recover/. Practitioner framing: rankings up + traffic down after a SERP feature = clicks absorbed by the engine, none to win back · ahrefs.com/blog/website-traffic-down. Counter-signal: Chartbeat says publisher search referrals hold · seotldr.substack.com. All of these are third-party aggregates about other people's sites; none transfers to yours without your own GSC before/after.

Core updates. Dec 2025 core + spam: described as small, hurting only sites that should not have ranked · https://seotldr.substack.com/p/googles-core-update-term-drift-merchant ; https://www.seroundtable.com/video-12-12-2025-40587.html. June/July 2025: ~3-week rollout, little volatility · https://seotldr.substack.com/p/junejuly-core-update-rolling-out. March 2026: scaled-content-abuse enforcement was its biggest component · digitalapplied.com. HCU recovery line across Google guidance and third-party posts: no panic changes, refresh + internal links + depth · dev.to/synergistdigitalmedia.

Reddit in the SERP. +1,328% US visibility Jul 2023 → Apr 2024; domain rank #85 → #5; 57M → 427M monthly organic visits; #2 by Aug 2025 · thestacc.com. The March 2026 core update dropped Reddit itself by more than 500 SISTRIX points (3,200 → 2,699), to #4 · https://www.techpluto.com/why-reddit-is-ranking-everywhere-on-google/ ; thestacc.com. Dominance by query class: gaming 98%, "best X" 87%, "is X worth it" 81%, troubleshooting 74%, finance/health 68%; weak on transactional 8%, branded SaaS 6% · thestacc.com. Reddit's rise coincided with independent editorial sites losing positions · techpluto.com. Practitioner-cited cause: the $60M/yr Google–Reddit licensing deal (Feb 2024) · https://www.sixthcitymarketing.com/2025/02/14/reddit-and-google/. The visibility, rank, visit and share figures in this paragraph are third-party SEO-tool estimates relayed by blogs `[vendor]`.

GEO / AI citations / llms.txt. Four camps across 38 threads (scam / harmless hygiene / get ahead of it / it's just SEO) · powerfulcombo.com. One site's server logs, Jul 2026: training crawlers fetched machine-readable files 177 times; ChatGPT-User 1,936 HTML pages, 0 machine-readable; PerplexityBot 306 / 0; Claude-User 38 / 1 — so the file reaches training crawlers but not live answers · same URL. Ahrefs, 137,210 domains (Jun 2026) `[vendor]`: 97% of llms.txt files get zero requests, 77% of readers aren't AI; SE Ranking `[vendor]` ~300k domains (Nov 2025): no correlation with citations; HTTP Archive top-10k adoption 5.61% · same URL. `[documented: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide, 2026-10-11]` Google: "Google Search itself doesn't use" llms.txt; "Structured data isn't required for generative AI search". ~48% of AI-search citations come from UGC/community sources (AirOps 2026) `[vendor]` · https://www.siegemedia.com/seo/reddit-seo-guide. John Mueller: "the higher the urgency, and the stronger the push of new acronyms, the more likely they're just making spam and scamming" · https://searchengineland.com/google-john-mueller-seo-geo-audience-behavior-467257. Mowei gate (Fable, `[practitioner]`): list which AI bots the Cloudflare policy allows before recommending anything aimed at them — a blocked bot reads nothing.

Site reputation abuse. `[documented: https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/08/update-site-reputation-policy, 2026-10-11]` From 30 Aug 2026, following discussion with the European Commission, a site reputation manual action is still issued and notified in Search Console, but its impact does not apply for searchers in the EEA; the affected section may instead be separated so it ranks independently. Outside the EEA the action still affects the affected portion. Portugal is in the EEA. This does not make parasite SEO advisable — policy risk outside the EEA, reputational and YMYL-correctness risk everywhere. Nov 2025: the European Commission opened DMA proceedings after a European Publishers Council complaint · capconvert.com.

Agentic-specific 2026 facts. MCP servers exist for every major SEO data tool as of May 2026; free `mcp-gsc` (AminForou) is the recommended GSC connector; Ahrefs, Semrush and DataForSEO ship MCPs · https://seoprofy.com/blog/best-mcp-server-for-seo/ ; https://www.get-ryze.ai/blog/connect-ai-agent-google-search-console.

## Tooling folklore

Tool threads are vendor-brigaded: affiliate links, founders answering as users, and "honest review" posts that are launches. These discussions surface overpriced tools fast but are weak on whether a tool is needed at all (mpgone.com). Read the positions below as what people say they use, not as benchmarks.

- Semrush or Ahrefs — pick one, for keyword and competitor research · mpgone.com
- Screaming Frog for crawling (near-universal); Sitebulb as the visual alternative · same
- GSC + Google Trends as the free essentials; on your own site, GSC outranks any paid estimate · same
- SE Ranking as the budget all-in-one (top answer, score-51 / 133-comment general-practitioner thread); warning against Search Atlas metrics in the same thread · same
- DataForSEO API ($50) + Claude subscription ($20) as the winning budget stack — a growth-community thread, score 64 / 149 comments / 25K views in 4 days · same
- LowFruits as a cheap Semrush alternative; PageCue as a $9 one-time crawler · same
- Tool-free camp: your judgement, later a more experienced one; Google/Bing tools + AWStats · same
- Honest tool reviews with stated limitations are a named strength of the general community · flow20.com
- GEO trackers people name: Profound (enterprise; $20M Series A Jun 2025), Peec AI (€89/mo tier), Otterly (which URLs get cited), ReddGrow (also tracks Reddit citations) · https://reddgrow.ai/blog/best-aeo-geo-tools-2026/ ; https://practicalecommerce.com/tools-to-track-genai-citations-sources — none is a substitute for the Bing Webmaster Tools AI Performance report `[documented: https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview, 2026-10-11]`, whose Average Cited Pages figure "does not indicate ranking, authority, or the role of any page".
- GummySearch / RedditList for finding subreddits by engagement · wellows.com
- Title-tag testing: GSC is enough to measure; SEO Scout / seoClarity for controlled splits · https://www.seoclarity.net/blog/title-tags-improve-ctr-conducting-page-title-test-15648/ — the measurement discipline is in `titles-and-serp-tests.md`.
- Agent environments named: Claude Code, ChatGPT Agents, Cursor, n8n, Zapier; MCPs: Ahrefs, GSC (`mcp-gsc`), GA4, WordPress, DataForSEO · ahrefs.com ; seoprofy.com ; https://n8n.io/workflows/9502-create-ai-powered-seo-audit-reports-with-dataforseo-and-claude/
- Open-source Claude Code SEO skill ("claude-seo": 25 sub-skills, 18 sub-agents; optional DataForSEO/Firecrawl) · https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-seo
